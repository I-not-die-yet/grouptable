# Founder Board lenses — working summary

Source: [upstream lenses.md](https://github.com/Jakeschincariol/founder-skill/blob/main/skills/founder-board/lenses.md). Read alongside the [upstream skill](https://github.com/Jakeschincariol/founder-skill/blob/main/skills/founder-board/SKILL.md). This is a concise method reference, not an installed plugin.

These are published-framework lenses, not simulations of the named authors or their endorsements.

## Offers

Based on Alex Hormozi's *$100M Offers*. Examine the buyer, severity of the problem, ability to pay, reachability and market evidence. Assess the desired result, confidence in obtaining it, waiting time and user effort. Look for a concrete valuable offer and proof; do not invent a guarantee or demand evidence.

## Monopoly

Based on Peter Thiel and Blake Masters' *Zero to One*. Examine the initial niche, meaningful differentiation, timing, distribution, team fit, durability and the underlying insight. Ask what prevents easy substitution and how initial customers could be reached. Distinguish an unproven advantage from proof that none can exist.

## Product

Based on Walter Isaacson's *Steve Jobs* and the public product record. Examine the main task, first-use experience, focus, unnecessary steps and handoffs. Work back from the user's experience to what the product must do well. Identify what to cut and what makes the complete workflow useful.
